Common questions

What’s included with the sign?

A remote control and control box are included.

How do I choose a size?

The inch sizes refer to the digits. Check the cabinet dimensions in the size chart against your opening, or ask us about your installation.

We take orders for Canada and the United States. We confirm the shipping cost and estimated delivery before you complete the order.

What does the warranty cover?

The two-year warranty includes free repair parts for covered issues and telephone assistance.

Request a bulk quote with your quantities, preferred sizes, and delivery destinations.
